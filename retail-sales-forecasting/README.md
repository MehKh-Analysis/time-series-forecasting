# 🛒 Retail Sales Forecasting (SARIMA)

Time series analysis of superstore sales data to uncover trend, seasonality, and cyclic patterns, and to forecast future sales.

## Approach

- **EDA:** structure and sequential behavior of sales over time
- **Decomposition:** separating trend, seasonal, and residual components
- **Modeling:** SARIMA (Seasonal ARIMA) to capture seasonal patterns and generate forecasts

- `retail_sales_sarima.ipynb`: full analysis
